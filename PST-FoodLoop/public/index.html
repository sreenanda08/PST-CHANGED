🔐 Trust, Safety & Food Hygiene Governance

FoodLoop strictly enforces FSSAI food safety regulations, 5-point hygiene declarations, and automated 1-click incident pauses.

🛡️ Mandatory 5-Point Safety Pledge

Every provider must digitally sign all 5 safety declarations before a listing can be published:

✅
1. Hygienic Handling

Food handled strictly with sanitized gloves, hairnets, and clean culinary tools.

✅
2. Proper Holding Temperature

Hot foods held above 60°C; cold foods refrigerated strictly below 4°C.

✅
3. Unserved Edible Surplus Only

Surplus food comes from kitchen preparation pans; never previously served to customers.

✅
4. Strict Redistribution Window

Surplus must be distributed within 2 to 4 hours of preparation.

✅
5. Transparent Allergen Disclosure

All common allergens (Dairy, Gluten, Nuts, Soy) explicitly tagged.
